# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Jewish couples seeking thoughtful marriage guidance, as well as family members or trusted people helping a couple find appropriate support.

## Product Purpose

Binyan Shalem connects couples with personalized, practical marriage support grounded in rabbinical guidance. Success means a visitor feels safe, respected, and confident enough to begin a private conversation or support the mission.

## Positioning

Professional marriage guidance that combines practical support, trusted professional referrals, and a Torah framework without charging couples for help.

## Operating Context

Visitors learn about the approach, submit a private intake form, text the organization, donate, or explore educational content about shalom bayit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public experience is a static website with Firebase-backed intake submissions and admin-created content resources.
- Donations are completed through the existing embedded Google Form; Venmo is not offered.
- The calm-blue frontend is the production design. The preceding production design is preserved on the remote backup branch documented in `ROLLBACK.md`.
- Shalom bayit resources use a baked-in frontend baseline plus Firebase-backed admin additions. Admin resources store links and metadata only; images remain remotely hosted and there are no image uploads.
- Existing factual copy, organization name, contact details, and intake terminology must be preserved.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Binyan Shalem / בנין שלם.
- Voice: compassionate, wise, calm, discreet, professional, hopeful, and never theatrical.
- The Torah framework is central and should be stated plainly.
- Existing logo asset: `Binyan Shalem_files/8594235c61f23b5d99cd2a2dbf37c8b5.jpg`.
- The landing concept uses warm, calming blues while preserving the existing logo as a recognizable brand asset.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing website copy and intake flow.
- Existing logo and brand assets in the project folder.
- A user-supplied mobile landing-page reference at `/Users/ikeyfranco/Downloads/IMG_1503.jpg`.
- No testimonials, outcome statistics, accreditations, or partner logos have been supplied; future designs must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Lower the emotional barrier to asking for help.
- Communicate professionalism through clarity, restraint, and care.
- Make the first next step unmistakable.
- Protect the dignity and privacy of every visitor.
- Keep educational content approachable and grounded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, readable contrast, semantic form labels, and responsive layouts for mobile and desktop.
